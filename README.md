شركاء الخير... معاً نساهم في صناعة الأمل ورعاية الأيتام.


**Khair Partners | Charity Contribution Management**

Khair Partners is a charity contribution management application designed to organize and track voluntary monthly donations for orphan sponsorship.

The app enables users to register contributors, manage monthly contributions, record cash and electronic payments, and track payment statuses. It provides detailed financial reports, individual contribution histories, and monthly and annual statistics.

Khair Partners also helps monitor sponsorship targets, calculate collected funds, identify remaining balances, and track surplus amounts allocated to gifts and additional charitable support.
